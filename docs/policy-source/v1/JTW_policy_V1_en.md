# JTW General Rules System V1.0
## English Version

# A. JTW General Travel Rules V1.0

## 1. Special Traveler and Safety Information

1. **Travelers aged 70 or above, pregnant travelers, and infants must be accompanied throughout the trip by an adult family member or adult companion.**

2. Travelers under 18 may not participate alone and must be accompanied throughout the trip by an adult guardian or adult companion.

3. Older travelers, pregnant travelers, travelers with limited mobility, and travelers with cardiovascular conditions or any other health condition that may affect travel safety should carefully consider whether the itinerary is suitable for them based on their own physical condition.

4. If a traveler requires special care, mobility assistance, or has any other circumstance that may affect normal participation, please inform our staff before booking so that we can confirm whether the itinerary is suitable.

5. Accompanying persons are expected to provide necessary care and assistance based on the traveler’s age, health, and mobility. Drivers and guides are responsible for the operation of the group itinerary and on-site coordination and cannot provide ongoing personal care, lifting or carrying assistance, medical care, or one-to-one caregiving.

6. During the trip, if the driver or guide determines that continuing a particular activity may present a clear safety risk based on the local environment or the traveler’s actual condition, the traveler may be advised to pause or refrain from that activity for safety reasons.

7. Medical expenses, emergency assistance costs, additional transportation expenses, and other personal expenses arising from the traveler’s own physical condition, failure to follow on-site safety instructions, or insufficient care by an accompanying person are, in principle, the responsibility of the traveler.

## 2. Child Participation and Pricing

1. **Infants under 2 years old may participate free of charge when they do not occupy a separate seat and are cared for by an accompanying adult.**

2. **Children aged 2 or above must occupy an individual seat and are charged at the adult rate.**

3. If an infant under 2 years old requires a separate seat, one standard seat will be charged.

4. If a child safety seat or other special arrangement is required, please inform us at the time of booking so arrangements can be made according to the vehicle and actual operating conditions on the day.

## 3. What Is Included

Unless otherwise stated on the specific product page, the product generally includes:

1. Vehicle service fees listed on the itinerary page.
2. Fuel costs and road/toll-related expenses for the itinerary vehicle.
3. Driver/guide service fees listed for the itinerary.
4. Other services expressly stated as included on the product page.

Vehicles are assigned by the operator based on actual participant numbers, route arrangements, and operating conditions on the day. **No specific vehicle model is guaranteed.**

## 4. What Is Not Included

Unless expressly stated as included on the product page, the following are generally not included:

1. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and other personal food and beverage expenses.
2. Admission fees and optional paid activities joined locally.
3. Personal shopping and other private expenses.
4. Transportation expenses incurred independently outside the itinerary.
5. Medical, emergency, additional accommodation, additional transportation, and similar expenses arising from personal circumstances.
6. Any other item not expressly listed under “What Is Included” or on the product page.

## 5. Meeting Point and Travel Notifications

1. This product departs from a designated meeting point. **Hotel or accommodation pickup and drop-off services are not provided.**

2. Please arrive on time at the meeting point and at the meeting time shown in your order and travel notification.

3. The meeting point, meeting time, and departure time are based on the order page and the latest notification sent before departure.

4. On the day of travel, please keep the mobile phone number and usual contact method registered at booking reachable so that you can receive meeting updates, itinerary changes, and other important notices.

5. If meeting information must be changed due to weather, road restrictions, or other operational circumstances, we will make reasonable efforts to notify travelers through order messages, system notifications, or the registered contact details.

## 6. Itinerary Rules

1. This product is a fixed group travel itinerary shared by multiple travelers. Please observe the designated meeting times at each stop and cooperate with the driver/guide’s on-site arrangements.

2. Actual transportation, sightseeing, and stop durations are subject to the day’s operating conditions.

3. To keep the overall trip running smoothly, the driver/guide may reasonably adjust the order of attractions and stop durations based on road conditions, weather, crowd levels, attraction operations, or other on-site circumstances.

4. During the itinerary, do not enter restricted areas, climb over barriers, or engage in conduct that presents an obvious safety risk.

5. If a traveler chooses to end the trip early or leave the group during the itinerary, please inform the driver/guide in advance. Unused subsequent services will be handled in accordance with the travel conditions and cancellation rules applicable to that order.

6. If a traveler seriously disrupts the group, threatens the safety of others, refuses necessary safety instructions, becomes uncontrollable due to intoxication, or otherwise materially interferes with the safe and normal operation of the trip, the operator may take necessary safety measures according to the circumstances.

## 7. Weather, Traffic, and Force Majeure

Typhoons, heavy rain, heavy snow, strong winds, earthquakes, floods, volcanic activity, major traffic accidents, road closures, severe congestion, public transport disruptions, restrictions imposed by government or relevant authorities, temporary closure of attractions, sudden public events, and other circumstances beyond reasonable control may result in:

- Delays
- Changes to the order of attractions
- Shorter stop durations
- Changes in transportation arrangements
- Inability to use certain facilities or participate in certain activities
- Partial changes to or cancellation of the itinerary

In such cases, **traveler safety, driving safety, and the overall feasibility of operating the trip** will be prioritized, and reasonable adjustments will be made based on actual conditions on the day.

Any cancellation, change, refund, or other handling will be determined according to the **travel conditions and cancellation rules applicable at the time of booking, as well as the services actually provided and those that could not be provided**.

## 8. Attractions and Third-Party Facilities

Some attractions, sightseeing boats, ropeways, scenic trains, hot springs, performances, amusement facilities, and other services are independently operated by third parties.

Their opening hours, operating status, ticket availability, or service content may be changed or suspended temporarily due to weather, maintenance, on-site restrictions, visitor volume, or similar reasons.

If a third-party facility becomes temporarily unavailable, the driver/guide will reasonably adjust the relevant itinerary based on actual conditions that day.

## 9. Seasonal Scenery and Natural Conditions

Cherry blossoms, autumn foliage, snow coverage, flowering periods, sunsets, night-view visibility, and other natural scenery are affected by temperature, rainfall, wind, and other natural conditions.

Images and descriptions on the product page are intended to present the route and travel experience and **do not constitute a guarantee of blossom timing, foliage conditions, snow levels, weather, or scenery on any specific date.**

If natural conditions are not at their peak viewing period, the trip will generally operate as scheduled.

## 10. Luggage

1. Because this product is a sightseeing itinerary, bringing large luggage is generally not recommended.

2. If you need to bring large or multiple pieces of luggage, please inform our staff in advance at the time of booking.

3. The number and size of bags that can be carried may be limited by vehicle space, the number of participants, and safety requirements on the day.

4. Large or excessive luggage that was not declared in advance may not be accepted if it cannot be safely loaded into the assigned vehicle.

## 11. Meals and Food

1. Unless expressly stated on the product page, meals are generally not included.

2. During free time or when dining independently, please follow the rules of restaurants, attractions, and related facilities.

3. Some restaurants may not allow outside food or may not permit guests to occupy a seat without ordering.

4. Travelers with food allergies, religious dietary restrictions, or other special dietary requirements should independently confirm relevant restaurant and food information. Staff may assist with communication within a reasonable scope, but cannot guarantee that every restaurant or food item can fully meet special dietary requirements.

## 12. Personal Belongings

Please keep passports, cash, bank cards, mobile phones, cameras, and other valuables secure.

During free time, sightseeing, meeting, and transfers, please check your belongings to help prevent loss, theft, or damage.

If an item is lost, the driver/guide may provide reasonable assistance, but recovery cannot be guaranteed.

## 13. Health and Emergencies

If a sudden illness, injury, or other health emergency occurs during the trip, the driver/guide will, within a reasonable scope, assist in contacting local medical providers, emergency services, or the traveler’s emergency contact.

If medical or safety matters require priority handling, the traveler’s own arrangements and the group itinerary may need to be adjusted accordingly.

Medical fees, emergency assistance fees, medication costs, additional transportation, or accommodation expenses arising from a traveler’s personal health condition are, in principle, the traveler’s responsibility.

## 14. Local Laws and Public Order

During the trip, please comply with Japanese laws, traffic rules, attraction regulations, and public order.

Do not carry items prohibited or restricted by Japanese law, and do not engage in behavior that may endanger yourself, other travelers, staff, or public safety.

Please respect local customs and the visiting rules of religious sites.

## 15. Safety Notice

Please participate within the limits of your own physical condition and the local environment.

For activities or environments involving the sea, sightseeing boats, snow, hot springs, mountainous areas, stairs, slippery surfaces, or other inherent risks, please follow the safety instructions of local staff, the driver/guide, and facility operators.

If staff require an activity to be paused, stopped, or adjusted for reasons related to weather, equipment, roads, or other safety concerns, please cooperate.

Please prepare suitable clothing and footwear, sun protection, cold-weather gear, rain gear, and other necessary items according to the season and route.

## 16. Transportation After the Trip

Return and finish times shown on the itinerary page are planned reference times.

Because road traffic, weather, and attraction operations are subject to uncertainty, **we do not recommend scheduling important transportation that cannot easily be changed immediately after the expected end of the trip**, such as international flights, Shinkansen trains, or long-distance buses.

If you arrange onward transportation independently, please allow sufficient buffer time.

## 17. Rule and Order Versions

These General Rules may be updated as needed for operations, safety, or service.

For products not yet booked, the currently published version applies.

For confirmed orders, the applicable basis will, in principle, be the **route content, meeting information, route-specific reminders, General Rules, service-time rules, and cancellation-policy versions displayed and applicable at the time of booking**.

If actual operational changes become necessary after booking due to weather, traffic, temporary attraction changes, or similar circumstances, travelers will be informed through order notifications, system messages, or other appropriate methods.

Later updates to the rules will not automatically replace the rule versions applicable to already confirmed orders.

# B. JTW Standard 10-Hour Service-Time Module V1.0

## Trip Service Time

This product is a travel itinerary designed to operate within approximately **10 hours**. To ensure driving safety, proper driver/guide work arrangements, and the smooth completion of the group itinerary, **extended service beyond the planned service time is generally not provided.**

Actual trip duration may differ from the original plan due to traffic congestion, weather, holidays, crowd control, road conditions, attraction operations, and similar factors.

If the overall itinerary is expected to exceed the standard service time, the driver/guide will prioritize **a safe return and the smooth operation of the overall itinerary**, and may reasonably adjust the **order of attractions, free time, and stop durations** according to actual conditions that day.

Arrival times and stop durations shown on the itinerary page are planned reference times. **Actual operating conditions on the day take precedence.**

### Short Notice for Product Pages

> **This itinerary is designed to operate within approximately 10 hours. Attraction order and stop durations may be reasonably adjusted due to traffic, weather, and on-site operating conditions. Extended service is generally not provided.**

# C. JTW Standard 24-Hour Cancellation Policy V1.0

## Cancellation and Refund Policy

1. **If a cancellation request is submitted more than 24 hours before the scheduled departure time, 100% of the paid trip price will be refunded.**

2. **If a cancellation request is submitted within 24 hours of the scheduled departure time, no refund will be provided.**

3. **No refund will be provided for cancellations made after the trip has started, or when a traveler is unable to participate due to personal reasons.**

The cancellation request time is determined by the time at which the system actually receives and records the request.

Once an order has been confirmed, the cancellation policy and version applicable to that order will be retained. Later changes to the cancellation policy will not automatically alter the conditions applicable to an already confirmed order.
